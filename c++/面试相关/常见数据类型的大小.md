| 数据类型             | 32 位系统 | 64 位 Windows | 64 位 Linux |
| -------------------- | --------- | ------------- | ----------- |
| `char`               | 1         | 1             | 1           |
| `signed char`        | 1         | 1             | 1           |
| `unsigned char`      | 1         | 1             | 1           |
| `short`              | 2         | 2             | 2           |
| `unsigned short`     | 2         | 2             | 2           |
| `int`                | 4         | 4             | 4           |
| `unsigned int`       | 4         | 4             | 4           |
| `long`               | 4         | 4             | 8           |
| `unsigned long`      | 4         | 4             | 8           |
| `long long`          | 8         | 8             | 8           |
| `unsigned long long` | 8         | 8             | 8           |
| `size_t`             | 4         | 8             | 8           |
| `ptrdiff_t`          | 4         | 8             | 8           |
| `intptr_t`           | 4         | 8             | 8           |
| `uintptr_t`          | 4         | 8             | 8           |

其中，`size_t`、`ptrdiff_t`、`intptr_t` 和 `uintptr_t` 的具体底层类型由实现决定，不能简单认为它们始终等价于 `unsigned long` 或 `long`。
